# Code of Conduct — Araneae

**Araneae: Your network should answer to you, not report on you.** This repository exists to build open, verifiable hardware and software that respect user privacy. This code of conduct protects the same principle in how we work together: no one should be excluded, intimidated, or exposed for participating.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Araneae project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others’ private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Intentionally violating others’ privacy outside the technical scope of the project (Araneae stands for privacy **as a right**, not as a weapon against participants)

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, associated chats, in-person or online events) and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the responsible maintainers (for example via a private issue or a channel indicated in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) when appropriate). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated fairly; the project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

Enforcement may include a temporary or permanent warning, removal of contributions, ban from public interaction, or other measures the team deems appropriate to severity and context.

## Alignment with project philosophy

- **No hidden telemetry in the name of “community security”:** monitoring must be transparent, documented, and minimized.
- **Open hardware and software** do not excuse disrespect among people; the opposite undermines the project’s purpose.
